How to Master

Keynote.io is simple to learn but challenging to master. Here's everything you need to know to start your journey to musical domination.

1️⃣

Name Your Territory: Enter a name for your starting land—this is your identity.

2️⃣

Pick Your Song: Choose from multiple musical tracks and instruments to customize your experience.

3️⃣

Play the Rhythm: Press Q, W, E, or R as notes reach the white alignment line to score hits.

4️⃣

Build Your Streak: Hit 4 notes for bonus tracks, 10 for orchestral layers, 20+ for maximum mix.

5️⃣

Expand Your Realm: Each successful performance grows your territory and increases your rank.
