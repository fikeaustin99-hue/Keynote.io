How to Play

Keynote.io combines music and strategy into one addictive experience. Here's how it works:

Choose Your Land: Enter your name and pick your starting territory.

Select Your Song: Pick from various musical tracks and instruments.

Play Rhythm: Press Q, W, E, or R as notes reach the white line.

Build Your Streak: Hit 4 notes to unlock additional tracks, 10 for the full mix, 20+ for ultimate power.

Expand Territory: Each successful song expands your land and increases your rank.
